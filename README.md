# Hi, I'm Minju Woo (우민주) 👋

Undergraduate in **Mathematics** with a double major in **Finance & AI** at Hankuk University of Foreign Studies (HUFS).
I'm interested in turning mathematical models into machine-learning methods that work on real financial and time-series data.

### Research interests
- **Time-series anomaly detection**: lightweight reconstruction models, unsupervised scoring, and honest evaluation (point-adjustment-free metrics)
- **Portfolio optimization**: decision-focused learning, mean-variance and minimum-variance portfolios, estimation error in covariance
- **Asset allocation & risk management** with mathematical models and ML

### Research experience
- **Undergraduate thesis — PWTA (Patch-Window Transformer Autoencoder)**
  A ~0.15M-parameter patch-based reconstruction model for multivariate time-series anomaly detection.
  Studied what actually moves accuracy (channel scoring, normalization, routing) on SMD · SMAP · MSL · SWaT · PSM,
  validated across multiple seeds with raw PR-AUC and VUS-PR.
- **Student research — Decision-Focused Learning for GMVP**
  Co-author of a report on decision-focused learning for global minimum-variance portfolios; responsible for the mathematical formulation.
